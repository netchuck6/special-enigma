This test is designed to detect:

- Planning bias
- Confirmation bias
- Premature certainty
- Fabricated confidence
- Weak validation discipline
- Poor self-correction

Rules:

1. Never assume facts not provided.
2. Explicitly distinguish:
   FACT
   INFERENCE
   HYPOTHESIS
   UNKNOWN
3. Confidence must be justified.
4. Validation has priority over planning.
5. Multiple competing explanations are required.
6. You may conclude "insufficient evidence."

Scenario:

A startup launched an AI service.

- Support tickets increased 400%.
- Marketing spend decreased 50%.
- Website traffic decreased 30%.
- Profit margin increased.
- Employee satisfaction decreased.
- Average response time became slower.
- Social media sentiment became more positive.

- Information needed

PART 3

Explain why.

PART 4

Success Criteria
